# About Me

Hey, I'm Shaun — a Computer Science graduate with a growing passion for cyber security, particularly the technical side of the field.

I hold a Bachelor of Information Technology, majoring in Computer Science, and I'm currently undertaking a Certificate IV in Cyber Security. I'm especially interested in web and AI penetration testing, as well as the emerging intersection between agentic AI and infrastructure security.

## What I'm Exploring

I'm constantly looking to expand my technical knowledge and challenge myself with new problems. At the moment, much of my personal development is focused on:

- **Penetration Testing:** Developing practical skills through TryHackMe's Junior Penetration Tester pathway.
- **AI Security:** Exploring AI security concepts, vulnerabilities and the security implications of increasingly autonomous systems.
- **Networking & Infrastructure:** Building a stronger understanding of networking, Linux and the underlying systems that make modern technology work.

I also undertake learning through Cisco Networking Academy, alongside independent research and hands-on experimentation.

## Community & Industry

I believe learning is strengthened by being involved in the wider community. I'm actively engaging with the cyber security industry through volunteering with **Cyber In Motion**, participating in CTF activities, and attending industry events and meetups, including those organised by AISA.

I enjoy connecting with people who share an interest in technology, exchanging ideas and learning from the experiences of others.

## Beyond Technology

Outside of cyber security, I enjoy puzzle and strategy games, exploring psychology and philosophy, and listening to technical cyber security conferences.

I find there's something particularly satisfying about solving complex problems, understanding how things work beneath the surface, and approaching challenges from different perspectives.

## What's Next?

I'm driven by curiosity and a genuine enjoyment of learning. Whether it's exploring a new vulnerability, understanding an unfamiliar technology or experimenting with emerging AI systems, I enjoy the process of figuring things out.

I'm looking forward to continuing to develop my technical capabilities, contributing to the cyber security community and exploring where the intersection of security and AI leads.

**For me, cyber security isn't just a career direction — it's something I genuinely enjoy doing.**
